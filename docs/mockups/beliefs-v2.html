What changes, and why

  1. Trust first. The top strip answers "should I believe any of this?": the verdict, the win rate against a random-entry baseline, whether conviction is calibrated, and what the shadow book holds. Today the honest answer is "unproven", and the page now says so.
  2. One ranked table instead of 21 identical cards. 17 of 21 cards say "long bias", even at 2% conviction, so the badge carried no information. A stance combines direction and conviction: Long above the 35% entry line, Leaning long, No view, or Excluded.
  3. Current price on every row, with stop, support and resistance drawn around it. The old card never showed the price, so "$374.64 stop" couldn't be read. Stop and invalidation were the same number on every name; they're now said once.
  4. Reasons in plain words, for and against. "Outperforming SPY by 4.3%" instead of indicator.relstrength +1.00, plus the strongest counter-argument. The expanded row shows the evidence balance as bars.
  5. Strict halal, not the old list. 7 of these 21 names fail the strict screen, but the old page showed them with a green shield. They're now marked Excluded and dimmed; after today's deploy the engine follows only strict-halal names.
  6. Context the operator actually uses: the core's weight in each name, the shadow's own position and P&L, and each name's decisions with a plain reason. A pre-market "sell" that couldn't have filled is flagged.
  7. Macro events once, not 20 times. CPI and GDP sat on every card; they're one calendar now, in ET.
  8. Removed: version numbers (v62700), "updated 5m ago" on every card, raw source keys, the regime label duplicated by confidence.
  9. Phone: a compact list with the verdict pinned on top; a row opens a sheet with the same detail as the expanded row.
